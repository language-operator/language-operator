---
description: Explore a topic in the codebase with a given persona
---

## Inputs

- $TOPIC — topic to explore
- $PERSONA (optional, default: go-engineer) — persona to assume; definitions in `requirements/personas/`

## Directions

- Assume the $PERSONA persona from `requirements/personas/$PERSONA.md`
- Put your hard hat on and explore $TOPIC
- Ask me if you have questions — this is a collaborative process

## Output

Results of an exploration ready to be converted to an implementation plan, if changes are necessary.
